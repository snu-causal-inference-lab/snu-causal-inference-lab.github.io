My research interests lie in causal inference, particularly **causal interaction** among multiple treatments and **effect modification**. I am also interested in **causal attribution**, **causes of effects**, and the **probability of causation**. More broadly, I aim to develop methods for these problems in complex settings and apply them to **empirical legal studies**.
